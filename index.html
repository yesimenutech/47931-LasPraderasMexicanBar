About Us

We are Las Praderas Mexican Bar & Grill

Est. July 4, 2025 • Oklahoma City

Since July 4, 2025, Las Praderas Mexican Bar & Grill opened its doors with the dream of sharing the very best of Mexican gastronomy—offering flavorful dishes, a welcoming atmosphere, and special moments to enjoy with family and friends.

With passion, dedication, and a steadfast commitment to delivering excellence every day, we continue building our story and crafting unforgettable dining experiences for every guest who walks through our doors.

Thank you for being part of Las Praderas Mexican Bar & Grill!
